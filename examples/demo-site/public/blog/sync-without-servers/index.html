How offline sync works without a central server

Every note in Lumen is a list of small changes. When two devices edit the same note while offline, both lists are kept and merged in the same order on every device.

Why not last write wins?

Because the last write is often the smaller one. A quick fix on your phone should not erase an hour of work on your laptop.

The trade-off is a little more storage per note, which we compact every night.
