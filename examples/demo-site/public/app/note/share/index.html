Interview notes: onboarding

Five people tried the new welcome flow. Three skipped the import step; two asked what a space is.

What we heard

  • "I just want to start typing." Everyone said a version of this.
  • The progress bar helped, but step 2 felt optional and long.
  • Invites made more sense after the first note, not before.

Next

Try a one-step welcome (see Welcome) and move invites to the share dialog.
